# 6. Go-Live

## 6.1 Go-Live Plan

### 6.1.1 Creating Go-Live Plan and schedule

| Owner | Time Duration (hours) | Assets (attach if available) |
|-------|-----------------------|------------------------------|
| IC    | 0.5                   | Go-Live Plan                 |

Prepare a time‑boxed go‑live runbook with pre‑push checks, production
push steps, post‑push verification tasks, stakeholder contacts and
rollback criteria. Define monitoring windows and the support roster for
the first 48–72 hours and include communication templates for status
updates and incident escalation. Confirm final pre-go‑live sign-offs for
any DPs, scheduled exports and FPDI jobs.
